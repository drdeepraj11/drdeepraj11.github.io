About

Research-driven and internationally experienced

A concise overview of my academic background, research interests and professional experience.

I hold a Ph.D. in Biochemistry from Yeungnam University, South Korea, completed under the Global Korea Scholarship. My research career has included academic and scientific experience across multiple international research environments.

My scientific interests are centered on understanding and engineering enzymes for applications in drug discovery, metabolism and biotechnology. A major focus of my work has been the cytochrome P450 enzyme system and its potential for studying human drug metabolism and developing biocatalytic platforms.

I am also interested in interdisciplinary approaches that connect biochemical research with microfluidics, membrane proteins, extracellular vesicles and emerging computational and AI-enabled biomedical technologies.
