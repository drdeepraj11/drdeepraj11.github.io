About Me

I am a biochemist and researcher with a Ph.D. in Biochemistry from Yeungnam University, South Korea, supported by the Global Korea Scholarship.

My research experience spans India, South Korea, China and the United States, with a strong focus on enzymes, drug metabolism, enzyme engineering and biocatalysis.

My work has particularly focused on cytochrome P450 enzymes, enzyme-mediated drug metabolism and innovative approaches for studying biochemical transformations.

I am also interested in translational biotechnology, interdisciplinary research and the development of technologies that connect fundamental biochemical research with pharmaceutical and biomedical applications.

Research

My research interests span enzyme biology, drug metabolism, enzyme engineering and translational biotechnology.

Cytochrome P450

Investigating cytochrome P450 enzymes and their roles in drug metabolism, biological function and human health.

Drug Metabolism

Studying enzyme-mediated drug metabolism and approaches for understanding enzyme-substrate interactions.

Enzyme Engineering

Developing and applying enzyme engineering strategies for biochemical and pharmaceutical applications.

Biocatalysis

Exploring enzymes as powerful catalysts for sustainable biochemical transformation and biotechnology.

Enzyme-Bag Platform

Developing innovative enzyme-based platforms for CYP biotransformation and high-throughput drug metabolism screening.

Translational Biotechnology

Connecting fundamental biochemical research with pharmaceutical, biomedical and translational applications.
